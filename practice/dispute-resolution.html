Our Dispute Resolution Practice

When disputes arise, Spring Legal Consultancy provides tenacious, strategic representation to protect our clients' interests. Our litigators bring decades of combined courtroom experience, handling disputes across the full range of civil and commercial matters.

We pursue resolution through the most effective route — whether that is litigation, arbitration, mediation, or negotiation — always with our client's commercial objectives firmly in view.

Areas of Expertise

  • High court civil and commercial litigation
  • Alternative dispute resolution (arbitration and mediation)
  • Debt recovery and enforcement proceedings
  • Contract disputes and breach of agreement claims
  • Employment and labour disputes
  • Constitutional and administrative law matters
  • Enforcement of judgments and court orders
  • Cross-border dispute resolution and international arbitration
