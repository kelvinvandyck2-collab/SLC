Civil Construction

Legal advisory and dispute resolution for contractors, developers, and infrastructure projects.

Our Civil Construction Practice

Construction projects involve complex contractual frameworks, significant financial exposure, and high-stakes disputes. Spring Legal Consultancy provides specialist legal advisory to contractors, subcontractors, developers, government agencies, and project financiers across Ghana's construction and infrastructure sector.

We are engaged from the contract drafting stage through project execution to post-completion dispute resolution, providing continuity of legal support throughout the project lifecycle.

Areas of Expertise

  • Construction contract drafting and negotiation (FIDIC, NEC, bespoke)
  • Subcontractor and supply chain agreements
  • Project finance documentation and security
  • Extension of time and delay claims
  • Payment disputes and adjudication
  • Defects liability and performance bond claims
  • Public procurement advisory
  • Construction arbitration and litigation

Why SLC?

Our team's experience spans both public and private sector construction projects. We understand the technical language of construction disputes and work closely with quantity surveyors and engineers to build legally robust cases for our clients.

Have a Construction Legal Matter?

Our team advises contractors, developers, and project owners at every stage of a project.
