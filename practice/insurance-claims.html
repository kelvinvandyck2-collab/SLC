Insurance Claims

Professional handling of insurance disputes and claims for corporate and individual clients.

Our Insurance Claims Practice

When insurers deny, delay, or underpay claims, Spring Legal Consultancy steps in to advocate for policyholders and ensure they receive what they are contractually entitled to. We also advise insurers and reinsurers on policy wording, coverage disputes, and regulatory compliance.

Our insurance practice spans the full range of insurance lines, including property, motor, liability, life, and marine insurance, with particular strength in aviation-related insurance matters.

Areas of Expertise

  • Property and casualty insurance claims
  • Motor vehicle accident and fleet insurance disputes
  • Aviation hull and liability claims
  • Life and health insurance disputes
  • Professional indemnity and directors' liability claims
  • Marine cargo and hull claims
  • Policy interpretation and coverage disputes
  • Subrogation and recovery actions

Why SLC?

Spring Legal Consultancy's combined expertise in aviation law, corporate law, and litigation makes us uniquely positioned to handle complex, multi-line insurance claims that span industry sectors. We have a strong track record of securing favourable settlements and judgments for both individual and corporate policyholders.
