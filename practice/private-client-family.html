Our Private Client & Family Practice

Spring Legal Consultancy handles the most sensitive personal legal matters with discretion, empathy, and expertise. Our private client team understands that these matters often involve deeply personal circumstances, and we provide counsel that is not only legally sound but also compassionate.

We guide individuals and families through significant life events — from drafting a will to navigating a complex divorce — ensuring their rights and interests are protected throughout.

Areas of Expertise

  • Matrimonial disputes and divorce proceedings
  • Child custody, maintenance, and access arrangements
  • Adoption proceedings
  • Wills, trusts, and estate planning
  • Probate administration and estate distribution
  • Succession disputes and inheritance claims
  • Maintenance and financial remedies
  • Prenuptial and cohabitation agreements

Why SLC?

Mrs. Ruby Akua Aglagoh, a Human Rights Advocate with over two decades of experience, leads our private client practice. Her background ensures that sensitive matters — particularly those involving vulnerable clients, children, or complex family dynamics — are handled with the highest level of care and professional judgment.
