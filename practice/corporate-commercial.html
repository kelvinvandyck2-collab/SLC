Our Corporate & Commercial Practice

Spring Legal Consultancy advises businesses — from start-ups to multinational corporations — on the full spectrum of corporate and commercial legal matters. We act as trusted counsel to boards, executives, and management teams across all sectors of the Ghanaian economy.

Our team understands that commercial transactions must be structured efficiently, with legal risks mitigated and business objectives kept at the forefront of every advisory mandate.

Areas of Expertise

  • Company incorporation, structuring, and registration
  • Corporate governance, board advisory, and compliance
  • Mergers, acquisitions, and joint ventures
  • Shareholders agreements and equity arrangements
  • Drafting and negotiating commercial contracts
  • Supply and distribution agreements
  • Regulatory compliance and licensing
  • Employment and labour law advisory

Why SLC?

Our managing partner, Alexander Nii Kwartey Owoo, brings over two decades of corporate law experience, including his tenure as Head of Legal at HFC Bank. This background gives SLC a uniquely commercial understanding of legal risk — enabling us to deliver advice that is both technically sound and commercially practical.
