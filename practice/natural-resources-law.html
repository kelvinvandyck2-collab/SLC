Natural Resources Law

Legal advisory for energy, mining, oil, and natural resource development projects in Ghana.

Our Natural Resources Law Practice

Ghana's natural resources sector — encompassing petroleum, minerals, and forestry — operates within a complex and evolving regulatory framework. Spring Legal Consultancy provides specialist legal advisory to resource developers, investors, government entities, and communities navigating this landscape.

We advise on every stage of the resource development cycle, from exploration licensing and environmental compliance to project finance and dispute resolution.

Areas of Expertise

  • Mining and petroleum exploration licensing advisory
  • Joint operating agreements and production sharing contracts
  • Environmental compliance and regulatory approvals
  • Community engagement agreements and benefit-sharing frameworks
  • Natural resources project finance and security documentation
  • Royalty and fiscal regime advisory
  • Mining and oil sector dispute resolution
  • Regulatory submissions to the Minerals Commission and GNPC

Why SLC?

As Ghana continues to grow as a significant energy and mineral producer, the legal frameworks governing these sectors become increasingly sophisticated. SLC's team stays at the forefront of regulatory developments — ensuring our clients are positioned to operate compliantly and capitalize on investment opportunities in this critical sector.

Involved in the Resources Sector?

Our team advises on licensing, compliance, transactions, and disputes in Ghana's resources industry.
