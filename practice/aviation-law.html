Aviation Law

Specialized regulatory and transactional legal support for the aviation sector.

Our Aviation Law Practice

Spring Legal Consultancy provides comprehensive legal advisory services to a wide range of aviation sector stakeholders — from airlines, aircraft operators, and maintenance organizations to regulators, financiers, and insurers operating in Ghana and across West Africa.

Our team brings deep expertise in the unique regulatory landscape governing civil aviation, combining legal knowledge with an understanding of the commercial realities of the industry.

Areas of Expertise

  • Regulatory compliance and advisory under the Ghana Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA) framework
  • Aircraft acquisition, financing, and leasing transactions
  • Aviation insurance claims and dispute resolution
  • Passenger rights, liability, and compensation claims
  • Ground handling agreements and airport service contracts
  • Airspace and slot allocation disputes
  • Aircraft accident investigations and litigation support
  • International aviation treaty compliance (Chicago Convention, Warsaw/Montreal)

Why SLC for Aviation?

Mrs. Ruby Akua Aglagoh, our founding partner, holds specialized expertise in aviation law — one of only a few practitioners in Ghana with this level of dedicated sectoral knowledge. Our firm has advised on aviation contracts, regulatory submissions, and enforcement matters, making us a trusted partner for operators navigating Ghana's evolving aviation landscape.
