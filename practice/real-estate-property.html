Real Estate & Property

Legal support for property transactions, leasing, conveyancing, and land dispute resolution.

Our Real Estate & Property Practice

Navigating Ghana's property landscape requires legal counsel with deep local knowledge and technical expertise. Spring Legal Consultancy advises buyers, sellers, developers, landlords, tenants, and financiers on all aspects of real estate law in Ghana.

From due diligence on land titles to complex development agreements, we ensure our clients' property interests are protected at every stage of the transaction.

Areas of Expertise

  • Land title investigations and due diligence
  • Conveyancing and property transfers
  • Lease drafting and negotiation
  • Commercial and residential property transactions
  • Land dispute resolution and litigation
  • Stool land and customary land advisory
  • Mortgage and security documentation
  • Property development agreements and joint ventures

Why SLC?

Our team has handled a diverse portfolio of property matters, from residential conveyancing to large commercial development projects. We understand the unique complexities of Ghana's land tenure system — including customary, freehold, and leasehold — and advise clients accordingly.

Have a Property Matter?

Our real estate team is available to assist with transactions, disputes, and advisory work.
